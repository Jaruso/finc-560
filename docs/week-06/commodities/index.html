Forecast methodology

Real, monthly averaged daily spot prices from EIA through FRED; not real-time futures or live tradable quotes. The current incomplete month is excluded.

Mean reversion: A bounded log-price AR(1) fitted to up to 60 historical months. Trend: Recent 36-month log-linear OLS trend with a ±3.5% monthly slope cap. Unchanged: The latest observed monthly average.

Scenario: The price shock is a hypothetical immediate percentage adjustment. It decays by half over the selected number of months. This is a sensitivity calculation, not a supply-demand forecast.

Corridor: Trailing monthly realized log-return volatility multiplied by the square root of the forecast horizon. Displayed bounds are illustrative, not a calibrated confidence interval or empirically validated forecast errors.

Source links and reporting currency belong to each selected commodity. Forecast calculations run locally in your browser.
