Data and methods

Historical revenue, operating income and net income come from the SEC's annual EDGAR Company Facts US-GAAP data, without interpolated quarters. Individual filing concepts and accession numbers are included in each published company snapshot.

Revenue forecasts use an OLS trend or a CAGR fitted to recent annual revenue. Operating and net margins use three-year recency-weighted historical averages. Growth adjustments compound relative to the selected baseline, while the margin adjustment changes both derived projected profit margins by the stated percentage points. This is a driver-based conditional projection, not a probability distribution or a complete integrated financial-statement model.

SEC filing dates, amendments, fiscal calendars and taxonomy changes can affect year-to-year comparability. Featured-company snapshots update when scheduled GitHub Actions successfully refreshes all companies. No API keys or simulated financials are included in the public site.
