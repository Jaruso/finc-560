Diebold–Li (fixed model)

The dashboard fits a fixed-decay Nelson–Siegel curve to eight synchronized Treasury maturities each month, producing level, slope, and curvature factors. Separate historical AR(1) regressions project these factors into future Treasury yields. This is a simplified two-stage Diebold–Li-style model; no alternative bond forecasting model is implemented.

The Fed-rate deviation applies a conditional scenario using an observed historical association between monthly policy-rate and yield-factor changes, not a causal estimate of a Fed decision. The historical-error ranges and rolling backtests characterize past forecast performance, not guaranteed future outcomes.

Model methodology & limitations

Observations: Eight FRED Treasury constant maturities (1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 20 and 30 years) and the effective federal funds rate. Each monthly yield cross-section is selected from a single common trading date. The latest month may be incomplete.

Forecast baseline: Fixed-decay Nelson–Siegel level/slope/curvature factor regression (λ = 0.0609 per month), followed by separately estimated monthly factor AR(1) forecasts. This is a simplified Diebold–Li-style approach, not an arbitrage-free model.

Historical-error ranges: 10th–90th percentile forecast errors estimated using expanding rolling-origin out-of-sample backtests at every horizon; joint spread errors are measured directly. Descriptive historical-error ranges do not guarantee future coverage, especially under policy scenarios.

Policy dial: One-time next-month DFF deviation relative to the statistical baseline. The fitted monthly association of changes in DFF with changes in the model factors is propagated using AR coefficients. This is observational association, not a measured causal impact of a surprise Fed decision or a futures-implied forecast.

Validation: 6, 12 and 24-month rolling out-of-sample RMSE compared with an unchanged-yields baseline. No future observations enter models fitted at earlier historical forecast origins. Partial current months are excluded from model training and backtests.

Refresh: Automated weekday FRED data/model refresh, not real-time market streaming. Failed refreshes retain the prior verified snapshot. Review model implementation.
